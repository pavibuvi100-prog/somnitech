About SomniTech

SomniTech helps you stay ahead of your wellness by visualizing live health metrics, tracking sleep quality, and generating shareable reports. The dashboard is built for simple health monitoring with a smooth real-time analytics experience.

Our mission

We make health monitoring approachable by combining intuitive controls, clear metrics, and dynamic visualizations. SomniTech is designed to help users understand trends over time and take quick action when it matters most.

What you get

  • Live vitals simulation: Heart rate, SpO₂, HRV, temperature, and sleep score all update based on your data.
  • Smooth trend charts: Curved graph lines make it easier to follow patterns and changes over time.
  • History & reports: Export data, generate printable reports, and share updates with caregivers or doctors.
  • Dark/light theme: Comfortable viewing for any environment.

How it works

Use the interactive sliders to enter or generate sensor readings, then press Start to begin monitoring. The dashboard stores recent history locally in your browser, animates the charts smoothly, and keeps your session active through simple authentication.
